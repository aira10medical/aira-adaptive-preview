What AIRA Adaptive does

Structured capture

Age, weight, height, smoking status, Caprini score, hypertension and planned procedure, validated before anything is calculated.

Official AIRA engine

Every result is produced by the versioned AIRA backend (Bukret Score model bukret_v1_2026), never by a chatbot or by this page.

Actionable report

Risk score and level, contributing factors, rule-based clinical recommendations and a surgical eligibility suggestion for physician review.

Private by design

Each professional works in an isolated account. No patient names or contact details are collected — only a short case alias.

AIRA Adaptive is algorithmic decision support for licensed professionals. Its suggestions require medical review and do not replace clinical judgment, examination or the treating surgeon's decision. Not for emergencies.
